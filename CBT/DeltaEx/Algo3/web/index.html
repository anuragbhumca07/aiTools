Algo Logic — Range Filter + KAMA Cloud v2 · Flag-candle trigger · PnL-based trailing
🌐 Zone Gate: BUY zone = BAR is GREEN (downward=0) AND p2/p3/hband/lband all ABOVE p1. SELL zone = BAR is RED (downward>0) AND all BELOW p1.
🚩 Flag Rule: while zone is active, first bar whose LOW touches KAMA cloud (low ≤ cloudTop for long / high ≥ cloudBot for short) is the FLAG. Flag low/high is a TRIGGER REFERENCE only — the actual SL is ATR-derived.
🎯 Entry Trigger: zone STILL green + close > p2 AND close > p3 (long, mirror for short). Fills at NEXT candle OPEN. Initial SL = entry ± 1.5 × ATR14. Qty (BTC) = ($150 / (1.5 × ATR14)) × sizeFactor, rounded to Delta contracts (0.001 BTC). SL hit ≈ $150 × sizeFactor loss. Entries skipped when ATR < 15. Opposite trigger while in trade → close & reverse.
🔁 Trail Rule (all thresholds × sizeFactor): at +$300 × sizeFactor unrealised PnL, SL jumps to lock $200 × sizeFactor. Every additional +$100 × sizeFactor PnL → SL locks +$100 × sizeFactor more. SL never retreats. Scanned every 1s via mark price.
▲ LONG entry
1. BUY zone active (GREEN bar + p2/p3/hband/lband > p1)
2. Flag candle — low ≤ cloudTop while zone is on
3. Trigger — close > p2 AND close > p3
4. Fill @ next candle OPEN
▼ SHORT entry
1. SELL zone active (RED bar + p2/p3/hband/lband < p1)
2. Flag candle — high ≥ cloudBot while zone is on
3. Trigger — close < p2 AND close < p3
4. Fill @ next candle OPEN
📈 TRAILING (PnL-based, 1s scan)
• +$300 PnL → trailing on, SL locks $200
• each +$100 PnL → SL locks $100 more
• lock = ⌊(bestPnL − 100)/100⌋ × 100; SL never retreats
🚪 EXIT — any trigger
• Initial SL — entry ± 1.5×ATR14 (1s scan)
• Locked SL — trailing stop after +$300
• Opposite trigger — close & reverse
• No fixed take-profit — trail is the only profit exit

Algo Logic — Range Filter + KAMA Cloud v2 · Flag-candle trigger · PnL-based trailing
🌐 Zone Gate: BUY zone = BAR is GREEN (downward=0) AND p2/p3/hband/lband all ABOVE p1. SELL zone = BAR is RED (downward>0) AND all BELOW p1.
🚩 Flag Rule: while zone is active, first bar whose LOW touches KAMA cloud (low ≤ cloudTop for long / high ≥ cloudBot for short) is the FLAG. Flag CLEARS immediately if zone drops. Flag low/high is a TRIGGER REFERENCE only — the actual SL is ATR-derived.
🎯 Entry Trigger: zone STILL green + close > p2 AND close > p3 (long, mirror for short). Fills at NEXT candle OPEN. Initial SL = entry ± 1.5 × ATR14. Qty = ($150 / (1.5 × ATR14)) × sizeFactor — SL hit = $150 × sizeFactor loss. Entries skipped when ATR < 15. Opposite trigger while in trade → close & reverse.
🔁 Trail Rule (all thresholds × sizeFactor): at +$300 × sizeFactor unrealised PnL, SL jumps to lock $200 × sizeFactor. Every additional +$100 × sizeFactor PnL → SL locks +$100 × sizeFactor more. SL never retreats.
⏱️ Timing: signal / new entry checked ONLY at candle close (:01s after boundary). Position SL & trailing scanned every 1s from entry.
▲ LONG entry
1. BUY zone active (GREEN bar + p2/p3/hband/lband > p1)
2. Flag candle — low ≤ cloudTop (touches P2/P3) while zone is on
3. Trigger — close > p2 AND close > p3 (outside KAMA cloud)
4. Fill @ next candle OPEN
SL = entry − 1.5 × ATR14 · qty = ($150 / (1.5 × ATR14)) × sizeFactor · SL hit = $150 × sizeFactor loss
▼ SHORT entry
1. SELL zone active (RED bar + p2/p3/hband/lband < p1)
2. Flag candle — high ≥ cloudBot (touches P2/P3) while zone is on
3. Trigger — zone STILL red + close < p2 AND close < p3
4. Fill @ next candle OPEN
SL = entry + 1.5 × ATR14 · qty = ($150 / (1.5 × ATR14)) × sizeFactor · SL hit = $150 × sizeFactor loss
📈 TRAILING PHASE (PnL-based, 1s scan)
• +$300 PnL reached → trailing activates, SL locks $200 profit
• Every additional +$100 PnL → SL locks $100 more (+$400→$300, +$500→$400, …).
• Lock formula: lockProfit = ⌊(bestPnL − $100) / $100⌋ × $100
• SL price = entry ± lockProfit / size; never retreats.
While trailing, tick log records only TRAIL-UPDATE and EXIT — normal per-candle ticks resume after exit.
🚪 EXIT — any trigger
• Initial SL — entry ± 1.5×ATR14 hit intra-candle (checked every 1s from entry)
• Locked SL — trailing stop crossed after +$300 activation
• Opposite trigger — close & reverse at trigger bar close
• No fixed take-profit — trailing stop is the only profit exit
Zones can flip between bars; flags reset the moment a zone breaks.

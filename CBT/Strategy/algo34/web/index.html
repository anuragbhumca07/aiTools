Algo Logic — RF bar-color zone · RSI(2) cross entry · 3-bar swing SL ref · ATR-based 3-phase trailing
🌐 Zone Gate (KAMA cloud removed): BUY zone = RF bar GREEN (downward=0). SELL zone = RF bar RED (downward>0). Nothing else — no p1/p2/p3 checks.
🎯 Entry Trigger (RSI(2) mean-reversion): BUY when RF bar is GREEN AND RSI(2) crosses ABOVE 10 (prev ≤ 10, curr > 10 — recovery from oversold). SELL when RF bar is RED AND RSI(2) crosses BELOW 90 (prev ≥ 90, curr < 90 — drop from overbought). Fills IMMEDIATELY at the current market price (open of the just-started candle) — no 1-bar deferral. Initial SL = entry ± 2 × ATR14. Qty = min(balance×1.5%, $150) / (2 × ATR14) — so SL hit = exactly $150 loss. Opposite trigger while in trade → close & reverse (also immediate).
🛡️ SL Rule: initial SL = entry ± 2 × ATR14 (fixed $150 max loss). Swing high/low from 3 bars is a trigger reference only, not the SL.
🔁 Trail Rule (3-phase, ATR-based; thresholds captured at entry): Phase 1 — hold initial SL until PnL ≥ 3×ATR. Phase 2 — SL to BREAKEVEN (locks $0). Phase 3 — at PnL ≥ 5×ATR, LOCK 3×ATR profit; then every +$100 PnL locks +$100 more. SL never retreats.
⏱️ Timing: signal checked at :01s past every candle boundary (self-correcting timer, no drift over long runs). Entry fires in the SAME tick — no 1-bar deferral. Position SL & trailing scanned every 1s from entry.
▲ LONG entry
1. BUY zone active — Range Filter bar is GREEN (downward=0)
2. RSI(2) crosses ABOVE 10 on the bar (prev ≤ 10, curr > 10 — recovery from oversold)
3. Fill IMMEDIATELY at current market (open of just-started candle)
SL = entry − 2 × ATR14 · qty = min(balance×1.5%, $150) / (2 × ATR14) · SL hit = $150 loss exactly
▼ SHORT entry
1. SELL zone active — Range Filter bar is RED (downward>0)
2. RSI(2) crosses BELOW 90 on the bar (prev ≥ 90, curr < 90 — drop from overbought)
3. Fill IMMEDIATELY at current market (open of just-started candle)
SL = entry + 2 × ATR14 · qty = min(balance×1.5%, $150) / (2 × ATR14) · SL hit = $150 loss exactly
📈 TRAILING — 3 phases (ATR-based, 1s scan)
• Phase 1 — bestPnL < 3×ATR → keep initial SL (entry ± 2×ATR).
• Phase 2 — Breakeven — at bestPnL ≥ 3×ATR, SL jumps to ENTRY price (locks $0).
• Phase 3 — Lock & Trail — at bestPnL ≥ 5×ATR, LOCK 3×ATR profit. Then every +$100 PnL beyond that → lock +$100 more.
• Lock formula (phase 3): lock = 3×ATR + ⌊(bestPnL − 5×ATR) / $100⌋ × $100
• SL price = entry ± lock / size; SL never retreats.
While trailing, tick log records only TRAIL-UPDATE and EXIT — normal per-candle ticks resume after exit.
🚪 EXIT — any trigger
• Initial SL — entry ± 2×ATR14 hit intra-candle (checked every 1s from entry)
• Breakeven SL — SL sitting at entry price hit after Phase 2 activation
• Locked SL — trailing stop crossed after Phase 3 activation
• Opposite trigger — close & reverse at trigger bar close
• No fixed take-profit — trailing stop is the only profit exit
